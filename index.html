Who I am & What I Build:
I’m Ryan Kevin Omondi Konyango (also known as 瑞安)—a backend software engineer and computer science undergraduate based in Hangzhou, China, originally from Zimbabwe. I specialize in designing scalable backend architectures, distributed task queues, and resilient REST APIs using Python, Django, FastAPI, Node.js, and PostgreSQL. I don't just write code to pass tests; I build modular, production-ready systems grounded in clean architecture and solid data flows.

My Dream & Why I Do It:
My ultimate ambition is to bridge cultures and solve real-world historical and social data representation challenges through software. I believe technology should preserve heritage, streamline human experiences, and empower communities. Whether I’m building a cultural preservation web platform or architecting automated data pipelines, my work is driven by a deep curiosity about how systems interact and a relentless drive to create things that genuinely matter.

How & Where I Do It:
Operating out of university housing in Hangzhou while collaborating across borders, I approach engineering through visual first-principles planning—mapping out complex data flows and state interactions before writing a single line of backend logic. I combine rigorous technical standards with a completionist mindset, leveraging Docker, Redis, Celery, and automated CI/CD pipelines to ensure everything I ship is robust, maintainable, and built to scale.

Goals & Ambitions:
I’m currently focused on engineering high-impact distributed systems, expanding my technical footprint across global tech ecosystems, and ultimately stepping into roles where I can architect large-scale backend infrastructure that touches millions of lives.

Open to all forms of employment, and available for freelance web and app design.
